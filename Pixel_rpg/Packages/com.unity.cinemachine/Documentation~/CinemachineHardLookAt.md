# Hard Look At

This CinemachineCamera **Rotation Control** behaviour rotates the camera to keep the **Look At** target in the center of the camera's frame. Optionally, an offset can be specified in order to look at a point that is offset from the LookAt target's origin.
This is useful for looking at a character's head, or some other point of interest.

### Properties

| Property           | Description                                                                                          |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Look At Offset** | Offset from the LookAt target's origin, in target's local space. The camera will look at this point. |
